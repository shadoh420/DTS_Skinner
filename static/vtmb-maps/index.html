Preview notesUnofficial Patch maps override stock. Lightmapped world surfaces, displacements, brush entities, props and pickups at their authored start state. Fog, the 3D sky camera, underwater fog and scrolling or animated textures are drawn; ropes, decals, sprites and glows are drawn, and rotating brushes that start on turn; dust motes and steam jets that start on are drawn (env_particle, func_particle and heat-wave steam are not); scripts and beams are omitted. Props and NPCs use an idle/reference pose, lit at their vertices from the map's lights and the lightmaps around them. Materials and brightness are approximate pending comparison with the game; the import report lists missing assets and skipped entities.
