Preview notesFree-flight preview without collision, gameplay or audio. Surfaces are drawn unlit, texture times vertex colour (the level's baked shading), as the conversions' Blender material script draws them, with the material names' clamp, mirror, two-sided, see-through and decal flags. Vertex alpha makes glass and water see-through. Chrome (environment-mapped) surfaces show their plain texture; there are no skies, fog, doors or characters beyond what the conversion holds.
