Skinner's own code is under the [MIT License](LICENSE). The content below is not.

**Starsiege: Tribes and Tribes 2.** The repository and the release data ZIP include converted previews of the stock
models and textures of Starsiege: Tribes (Dynamix / Sierra, 1998) and Tribes 2 (Dynamix / Sierra, 2001): the
Tribes 1 textures and model JSONs under `static`, the Tribes 2 textures and model JSONs under `static/t2`, the
Tribes 1 animation caches, and thirteen stock Tribes 1 shapes under `tools/dts_files` used by the tests. They
belong to the games' rights holder and are included only so that Skinner works out of the box with the games'
free 2004 releases. They are not licensed under the MIT License, and they will be removed from the repository and
the releases on request from the rights holder (open an issue on GitHub).

**Every other game** (Quake 3, Unreal, Unreal Tournament, Reflex Arena, Diabotical, Starsiege, Earthsiege 1 and 2,
Red Baron 3D, the Tribes conversion packs and mods) is read from your own install when you import it. Nothing
from those games ships with Skinner.

**N64 level conversions** are L. Spiro's work, used with permission; none ship with Skinner.

**Vendored code** (exogen's t2-mapper, Three.js, es-module-shims and others) keeps its own licence; see
`static/t2-maps/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt` and the headers of the files under `static`.
